# Code of Conduct

LM Atelier contributors must make participation respectful, constructive, and
welcoming.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and disagreements without personal attacks.
- Give actionable feedback and assume good intent.
- Respect privacy, boundaries, identities, and differing levels of experience.
- Credit other people's work and accept correction.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not acceptable in project
spaces or when representing the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may warn, temporarily restrict,
or permanently exclude participants whose conduct is harmful.

Report conduct concerns privately through contact information published on a
repository maintainer's GitHub profile. Do not put sensitive personal details in
a public issue. For threats or abuse that require platform intervention, use
[GitHub's report abuse process](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse).

Maintainers will limit report details to the people needed to review and respond
to the concern.
